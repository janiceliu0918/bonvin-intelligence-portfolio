ARCHITECTURE · HOW IT WORKS

One governed data layer, multiple business surfaces.

The application, reporting workflows and AI layer reuse the same normalized data and business services. This reduces duplicated logic and keeps AI answers grounded in the operational system.

ENGINEERING DECISIONS

Built for reliability, control and handoff.

  • Reusable business/query logic shared across UI and AI surfaces.
  • Controlled AI access through approved tools and structured data.
  • Reconciliation and validation for ingestion and reporting.
  • Progressive migration from pricing/Streamlit workflows to React + FastAPI.
  • Architecture, testing, deployment and handoff documentation for maintainability.
